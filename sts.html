Professional Summary

STS professional profile

I am a Master Mariner with extensive practical involvement in ship-to-ship (STS) operations involving product tankers, bunker vessels and FSO units. My STS experience includes offshore bunkering and transfer programmes in West Africa and the Caribbean, with over 300 operations across my seagoing career.

My STS Experience Logbook records assignments in the combined roles of Master / STS Superintendent and Master / Mooring Master. This operational background covers the planning, coordination, vessel approach and mooring phases of transfers, as well as cargo transfer supervision and marine safety.

My broader maritime background includes command of oil tankers and a VLCC, Harbour Master positions, and fleet operations responsibilities. These roles have strengthened my approach to risk management, communication and the coordination of complex marine operations.

Professional focus: Mooring Master · STS Superintendent · POAC opportunities, drawing on hands-on STS operations and Master Mariner experience.

What STS operational experience do you have?

My STS experience spans more than 300 operations involving oil product tankers, bunker vessels and FSO units, with operations in West Africa and the Caribbean. The figure is based on my STS competency statement; selected operations and assignment periods are documented in my STS Experience Logbook.

Have you performed Mooring Master or STS Superintendent functions?

Yes. My STS Experience Logbook records assignments described as Master / STS Superintendent in Luanda Bay during 2007–2008 and Master / Mooring Master during offshore operations in 2009 and 2014–2015.

What tasks can you support during an STS transfer?

My operational experience includes Joint Plan of Operation preparation, risk assessment, mooring and fendering arrangements, approach manoeuvre supervision, environmental monitoring and cargo transfer oversight.

What is your background beyond STS operations?

I am a Master Mariner (STCW II/2) with experience commanding tankers and offshore vessels, including a recent VLCC assignment. I have also served as Harbour Master and worked in fleet operations and marine safety.

Are you available for POAC, STS Superintendent or Mooring Master opportunities?

I am open to discussing international assignments involving Mooring Master, STS Superintendent and Person in Overall Advisory Control (POAC) responsibilities, subject to the project requirements and any applicable client approval or qualification criteria.
